    How to read this. RAM is memory held only by Gaus (private working set); memory shared with other programs isn't counted.     Guide
    Everything Gaus does, in two minutes.
    
          
        
    
        
    
          
    What Gaus does
    
            
    Gaus keeps your games updated when they patch, so they're ready when you want to play. It opens the launcher during an update window, follows the download, and can power the PC down afterwards.
    
          
    Get started
    
            
      
              
    1. Go to Settings › Accounts and launchers and add your launcher.
      2. 
              
    2. In Library, choose Add a game.
      3. 
              
    3. Select the game, choose Schedule and set when it usually patches, such as Thu 11:00. Times are South African time (SAST).
      4. 
              
    4. In the launcher, set the game to update automatically.
      5. 
            
    
          
    Downloads
    
            
    Start a Steam update yourself and Gaus picks it up on its own, for games in your Library. It appears in Downloads and in the bar along the bottom. You can also select a game and choose Track download.
    
            
    Speed is shown in Mbps, as Steam reports it. The downloaded size comes from Steam's own count, which it updates every few minutes, so Gaus uses the speed to fill the gap. Other launchers don't share their progress, so for those Gaus only shows that they're busy.
    
          
    Screen saver
    
            
    While a download runs, show its art full screen with the progress, darken the screen, and dim or black out your other monitors. Your launcher is minimized and keeps downloading. Click or press Esc to leave. The monitors stay powered, and Gaus can't change your room lights.
    
            
    To use your own pictures, go to Settings › Screen saver and choose My pictures. Gaus art is the default.
    
          
    Patch notes
    
            
    Select a game and click the Latest patch pill on its art to see the highlights of its latest updates, newest first, with a link to read each one in full. For Marvel Rivals, changes are also grouped by character, using your own picture of them if you've added one. Each gets a Buffed, Nerfed or Mixed badge, and a cog means a bug was fixed. Gaus works these out from the wording, so if a badge looks wrong, the full notes have the final say. They come from Steam, so other launchers' games don't have them yet.
    
          
    Your favourite character
    
            
    Select a game and choose Choose favourite. Pick a character and search for art with the site buttons. Drag a picture you like into the box, or copy it and press Ctrl+V. If a site asks you to sign in, use your own account in your browser first. Pictures become the game's art and screen saver. With no favourite, the official art changes each time Gaus starts.
    
          
    Powering down
    
            
    Go to Settings › When updates are done to choose shut down, sleep or hibernate. Downloads you start yourself only count if you tick Also after downloads I start myself.
    
            
    Gaus waits until nobody has used the PC for a while, then counts down. Moving the mouse cancels it. If the PC stays in use, it stays on. Open programs are closed, so save your work.
    
          
    If something looks wrong
    
            
      
              
    • A game won't update: in Steam, open the game's Properties › Updates and turn on automatic updates.
      • 
              
    • The PC doesn't wake: use the wake test in Settings › Waking the PC. A PC that's fully shut down can't wake itself.
      • 
              
    • Can't find Gaus: it keeps running in the taskbar's hidden-icons area.
      • 
              
    • Anything else: the Log shows what Gaus did and when.
